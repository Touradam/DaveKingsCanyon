About the Land

At the Snowline. On the Scenic Byway. Minutes from the Parks.

420 acres in three parcels with so many possibilities. At roughly 4,500 feet of elevation, the parcels sit along the Kings Canyon Scenic Byway, the 50-mile route known as the Gateway to the Sierras. Fresno is 38 miles to the west. The entrance to Kings Canyon and Sequoia National Parks is only 8 miles away….

Warm, dry Mediterranean summers give way to mild winters with occasional foothill snow. Oak woodlands, chaparral slopes, ancient manzanita, and massive granite outcrops define the terrain. Aerial views reveal ridge lines that look out over layered valleys, a mountain ideal for multiple houses or a private estate, and enough flat land to build a grand resort, glamping, and campgrounds. There is an option for a boutique business with a small self-service dry campground and a beautiful caretaker’s residence.

Just beyond the property: General Grant Grove and the Nation’s Christmas Tree is 11 miles away; Kings Canyon, one of the deepest canyons in the country, with the Kings River, Boyden Caverns, and Cedar Grove at its base. Sequoia National Park, home to the General Sherman Tree, the largest tree in the world, is just 36 miles away on the Generals Highway. National parks, giant sequoia groves, waterfalls and caves are all within easy reach of the property.

Recreational zoning, miles of highway frontage, and proximity to two national parks position this land as a credible candidate for tourism, hospitality, or a destination resort.

Development Concepts

Multiple Paths for Development

Sample concepts illustrate what this land can support. These are starting points for a vision, not finished plans. Select any rendering to explore it further.

Grand Resort

Kings Canyon National Park draws more than 800,000 visitors each year, enough traffic to support a large destination resort. The National Park Service has removed private lodges within the park and replaced them with rustic accommodations.

The property offers buildable acreage, miles of highway frontage, proximity to the park entrance (8 miles), access to a known aquifer, and electrical power in place. A destination resort here would face limited nearby competition. This rare private recreational-zoned land has all the attributes needed for a luxury complex.

Glamping Resort

Imagine outdoor hotel rooms which offer privacy in natural settings under an open sky with spectacular views. A king bed, a private bath, and hotel-level comfort in an outdoor setting.

Campground

Individual campsites can nest into brush and manzanita, screened from one another so guests enjoy privacy, quiet, and a direct connection to the landscape. The ease of direct highway access allows for all sizes and types of camping vehicles to be accommodated. This campground could be a full-service operation with electric, water, and sewer hookups, or it can be limited to a dry campground without any need for this additional infrastructure.

Hybrid Model: Small Campground + Luxury Caretaker’s Residence

For buyers seeking a manageable business paired with a private residence, this concept combines a small five-to-ten dry-site, self-service campground with a caretaker’s home that carries no legal size limit. Dry camping means no electrical, water, or sewer hookups. Self-contained campers need only a level, safe place to park for the night. Payment can be handled online or through a drop-box. No campfires would be allowed. Maintenance stays simple: routine cleaning around the sites and occasional trash pickup. The campground would sit near the highway entrance, while the residence could be built anywhere else on the property, including atop the ridge overlooking the beautiful San Joaquin Valley.

Private Estate

Imagine a custom built estate on the top of Sontag Point with amazing views of the San Joaquin Valley below. The ultimate private getaway with hundreds of acres to explore in private. This luxury home would be sited on the 240 acre parcel allowing for the remaining 180 acres to be reserved as a future investment for development.

Development Potential

Scale, Access, and a Landscape That Sells Itself

Four hundred twenty acres with miles of road frontage on one of California’s most scenic highways put scale, visibility, and natural character into a single holding. The photos and renderings here are entry points, not ceilings. Every concept builds on the same strengths: privacy within oak, chaparral, and manzanita woodlands, granite ridges with valley views, and an ideal location at the gateway to Kings Canyon and Sequoia National Parks.

The land can support eco-tourism, upscale glamping, a destination resort with guest accommodations, corporate retreats, or gateway lodging for park visitors. This land can also become a private playground/estate for someone that wants to enjoy a personal relationship with the mighty Sierras. For investors and developers, highway exposure, scenic highway traffic, and nearby recreation destinations combine into a long-term opportunity worth serious consideration.
