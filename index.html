About the Land

At the Snowline. On the Scenic Byway. Minutes from the Parks.

420 acres in three parcels with so many possibilities. At roughly 4,500 feet of elevation, the parcels sit along the Kings Canyon Scenic Byway, the 50-mile route known as the Gateway to the Sierras. Fresno is 38 miles to the west. The entrance to Kings Canyon and Sequoia National Parks is only 8 miles away….

Warm, dry Mediterranean summers give way to mild winters with occasional foothill snow. Oak woodlands, chaparral slopes, ancient manzanita, and massive granite outcrops define the terrain. Aerial views reveal ridge lines that look out over layered valleys, a mountain ideal for multiple houses or a private estate, and enough flat land to build a grand resort, glamping, and campgrounds. There is an option for a boutique business with a small self-service dry campground and a beautiful caretaker’s residence.

Just beyond the property, 11 miles away, lies the General Grant Grove and the Nation’s Christmas Tree. The Kings Canyon, one of the deepest canyons in the country, has the Kings River, Boyden Caverns, and Cedar Grove at its base. Sequoia National Park, home to the General Sherman Tree, the largest tree in the world, is just 36 miles away on the Generals Highway. National parks, giant sequoia groves, waterfalls and caves are all within easy reach of the property.

Recreational zoning, miles of highway frontage, and proximity to two national parks position this land as a credible candidate for tourism, hospitality, or a destination resort.

Grand Resort

Kings Canyon National Park draws more than 800,000 visitors each year, enough traffic to support a large destination resort. The National Park Service has removed private lodges within the park and replaced them with rustic accommodations.

The property offers buildable acreage, miles of highway frontage, proximity to the park entrance (8 miles), access to a known aquifer, and electrical power in place. A destination resort here would face limited nearby competition. This rare private recreational-zoned land has all the attributes needed for a luxury complex.

Glamping Resort

Imagine outdoor hotel rooms which offer privacy in natural settings under an open sky with spectacular views. A king bed, a private bath, and hotel-level comfort in an outdoor setting.

Campground

Individual campsites nestled into brush and manzanita, screened from one another so guests enjoy privacy, quiet, and a direct connection to the landscape. The ease of direct highway access allows for all sizes and types of camping vehicles to be accommodated. This campground could be a full-service operation with electric, water, and sewer hookups, or it can be limited to a dry campground without any need for this additional infrastructure.

Hybrid Model: Small Campground + Luxury Caretaker’s Residence

For buyers seeking a manageable business paired with a private residence, this concept combines a small five-to-ten dry-site, self-service campground with a caretaker’s home that carries no legal size limit. Dry camping means no electrical, water, or sewer hookups. Self-contained campers need only a level, safe place to park for the night. Payment can be handled online or through a drop-box. No campfires would be allowed. Maintenance stays simple: routine cleaning around the sites and occasional trash pickup. The campground would sit near the highway entrance, while the residence could be built anywhere else on the property, including atop the ridge overlooking the beautiful San Joaquin Valley. There are also potential tax advantages to owning a home that is part of a business.

Private Estate

Imagine a custom built estate on the top of Sontag Point with amazing views of the San Joaquin Valley below. The ultimate private getaway with hundreds of acres to explore in private. This luxury home would be sited on the 240 acre parcel allowing for the remaining 180 acres to be reserved as a future investment for development.

Google Search for Tax Advantages

Search question

What are the tax advantages to having a caretaker’s residence associated with a campground business in the state of California and the county of Fresno?

Research summary for a caretaker residence tied to a campground business: income tax exclusions, payroll tax savings, and local tax exemptions. This is general research, not tax advice.

Tax-free housing and business deductions

  • Convenience of the Employer Doctrine Primary federal and state doctrine that can allow a business to provide tax-free housing while deducting the related expenses. California Franchise Tax Board conforms to IRC Section 119.
  • Tax-free housing benefit If a caretaker is required to live on-site for maintenance and security, the fair market value of that lodging can be 100% excluded from the caretaker’s gross income.
  • Payroll tax exemption Because the lodging is non-taxable, neither the business nor the employee generally owes federal payroll taxes (FICA/FUTA) or California payroll taxes (UI/ETT/SDI) on the value of the housing.
  • Business expense write-offs The campground business can fully deduct ordinary and necessary costs of the residence, including utilities, maintenance, insurance, and structural depreciation.

Three IRS tests to qualify

  1. 01
    Business Premises Test The residence must be physically located on the campground property.
  2. 02
    Convenience of the Employer Test There must be a substantial business reason for the caretaker to live on-site, such as 24/7 emergency response, late-night guest check-ins, or continuous security.
  3. 03
    Condition of Employment Test The caretaker must be contractually required to accept the lodging to perform their duties properly.

California property tax and depreciation

  • Accelerated depreciation If the caretaker residence is a Park Model RV or manufactured structure, it may qualify for accelerated depreciation or cost segregation, allowing a large percentage of the asset’s cost to be written off in the first few years.
  • Homeowners’ Exemption If you are the owner-operator and the caretaker residence is your primary home, the California Homeowners’ Property Tax Exemption can reduce assessed value by $7,000.

Fresno County-specific considerations

  • Transient Occupancy Tax exclusion Fresno County enforces a 12% Transient Occupancy Tax on short-term campground and RV site rentals in unincorporated areas. Because caretaker housing is long-term residency (30+ consecutive days), it is entirely exempt from TOT.
  • Business property reporting Business-owned personal property inside the caretaker residence (appliances, tools, furniture) must be reported to the Fresno County Assessor on Form 571-L. Those items are taxable but depreciable, which can lower net business income tax over time.

Research overview only. Confirm any structure with a qualified tax professional.
